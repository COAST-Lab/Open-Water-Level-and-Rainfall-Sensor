# Open Water Level and Rainfall Sensor
## ℹ️ About this Repository
This repository provides information on [firmware](https://github.com/COAST-Lab/Open-Water-Level-and-Rainfall-Sensor/tree/053ae7c9415e82eb4ce25704e3add3affef4214a/Firmware), [fabrication](https://github.com/COAST-Lab/Open-Water-Level-and-Rainfall-Sensor/tree/053ae7c9415e82eb4ce25704e3add3affef4214a/Fabrication), [data analysis](https://github.com/COAST-Lab/Open-Water-Level-and-Rainfall-Sensor/tree/053ae7c9415e82eb4ce25704e3add3affef4214a/Data%20Analysis), and [educational resources](https://github.com/COAST-Lab/Open-Water-Level-and-Rainfall-Sensor/tree/053ae7c9415e82eb4ce25704e3add3affef4214a/Education) for the novel open water level and rainfall sensor (OWLR)! 

## 🔌 Introduction to the Sensor
This sensor utilizes the [XL-MaxSonar-WRLA1 MB7076 Waterproof Ultrasonic Distance Sensor](https://www.adafruit.com/product/1137) and [DFRobot Tipping Bucket Rainfall](https://www.dfrobot.com/product-2689.html?gad_source=1&gad_campaignid=22388643497&gbraid=0AAAAADucPlCge_eiHbrbfSSFHXEPQpZts&gclid=Cj0KCQjwndHEBhDVARIsAGh0g3B9x7FTVeO9jL-XrZRNZ8TjIOYHPVgGPCdZUj51mCrzeZ_KvEh1rmUaAgSEEALw_wcB) sensors alongside Particle Boron for a novel, low-cost (Under $400 US) water level and rainfall sensor that utilizes cellular and solar power! This sensor was also integrated into a high school classroom experience, resources that can be accessed in this repo. There are flooding and retention pond specific lesson plans and activities that can be utilized in a variety of classroom and environmental settings, not just the coast! 

## 🌟 Goals 
    - Sensor Design: Have a well validated and easy to build open source low cost water level and rainfall sensor
    - Education: Formulate lesson plans that utlize experiential and place-based learning. Engage students through the creation and deployment of their own sensor.
